# Onboarding and Benefits

## Employment terms
The probation period lasts 3 months. After probation, the notice period is 1 month.

## Learning and wellness
Each employee has a learning budget of €1,200 per year for courses, books, and conferences. The wellness allowance is €25 per month.

## Health insurance
Health insurance covers the employee and their dependents. The company pays 80% of the premium.

## Referral program
The referral bonus is €2,000, paid after the referred hire completes 6 months at the company.

## Reviews
Performance reviews take place twice a year, in June and December. Salary reviews happen once a year in January.
