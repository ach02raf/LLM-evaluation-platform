# Travel and Expenses Policy

## Submitting expenses
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense. A receipt is mandatory for any expense above €25. Reimbursements are paid within 10 business days.

## Approval limits
Managers can approve expenses up to €500. Expenses above €500 require director approval.

## Business travel
Employees receive a meal allowance of €60 per day on business trips. Hotels are capped at €150 per night. Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours. Business class is allowed for longer flights with director approval.
