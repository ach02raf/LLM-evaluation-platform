# Vacation and Leave Policy

## Annual vacation
Full-time employees receive 25 paid vacation days (annual leave) per calendar year. Vacation days accrue monthly.
Vacation requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance. Managers approve or decline requests within 3 business days.

## Carry-over
Employees may carry over up to 5 unused vacation days to the next year. Carried-over days must be used before March 31, otherwise they expire.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. A medical certificate is required after 3 consecutive sick days.

## Parental leave
The primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave.

## Public holidays
The company observes 11 public holidays per year.
