# Remote Work Policy

## Hybrid schedule
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Everyone must be reachable during core hours, from 10:00 to 16:00 local time. Fully remote work requires approval from a VP.

## Home office support
New employees receive a one-time home office stipend of €500 for equipment. The company also reimburses internet costs up to €30 per month.

## Working from abroad
Employees may work from abroad for up to 20 days per year. Both the manager and the security team must approve the request in advance.
