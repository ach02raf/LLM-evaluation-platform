# Information Security Policy

## Accounts and passwords
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory on all company accounts.

## Devices
All laptops use full-disk encryption. USB storage devices are prohibited on company laptops.

## Incidents and training
Any security incident must be reported to the security team within 1 hour. Every employee must complete security training within 30 days of joining, and then once per year.
